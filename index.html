State tournament

All varsity games count here, league and non-league. The CIAC changed how it seeds the tournament this year.

How to qualifyPlay at least 10 games and finish at .400 or better. Win pct = (2 × wins + ties) ÷ (2 × games). Only the first two games against the same opponent count.
How seeds are setNew for 2026: a power ranking that combines a league strength multiplier with each team's own results. The formula is not published, so this page shows the CIAC's own number.
Key datesLast day a game counts: Mon Nov 2. Pairings posted Tue Nov 3. Class S first round Nov 6–7, final Nov 21–22. All playoff dates.

Playoff dates

The Shoreline tournament runs in late October and finishes Nov 2. The CIAC state tournament starts right after.

Shoreline tournament

The top 2 in the regular-season standings get a first-round bye.

  1. QuarterfinalsTue, Oct 27*Higher seed hosts
  2. SemifinalsFri, Oct 30Higher seed hosts

*The Shoreline's page lists the quarterfinals as "Thursday, October 27", but Oct 27, 2026 is a Tuesday. Check with the league before making plans.
