How the picks are made

Seeds follow the CIAC power points in the latest rankings snapshot. Each game's odds come from the gap in power points; a tie after regulation is treated as a coin flip in overtime or penalties. The table runs the whole bracket 20,000 times.

What will change

  • Seeds are set from the final rankings after Mon, Nov 2. Pairings post Tue, Nov 3.
  • Bolton, Aerospace and Housatonic can still drop out; East Windsor, Terryville and a few others can still get in. That moves the byes and every line below them.
  • Many teams are within a tenth of a point, so a single result can swap seeds.

Shoreline-vs-Shoreline games

Four projected games are all-Shoreline: Haddam-Killingworth vs Hale Ray in round 1, Valley vs Morgan in round 2, then Cromwell vs Haddam-Killingworth and Cromwell vs East Hampton. A model built from this season's Shoreline scores agrees with the Haddam-Killingworth and Cromwell picks, but it would take Morgan over Valley about 2 to 1; on CIAC points that game is a coin flip, so the bracket's Valley pick is the shakiest one shown. East Hampton did beat Cromwell twice this season, both 2-1.
